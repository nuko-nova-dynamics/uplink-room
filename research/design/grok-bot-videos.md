# Grok Bot, from its own videos

What Grok Bot's interface does, learned from 38 videos (2026-11-17): every Grok Bot video on Matt Palmer's channel from Sept to Nov 2026 (23, about 2.5 hours) and every Grok Bot video on xAI's @grok channel (14 sessions and the trailer, about 12 hours). Each video was reviewed from its scene-change frames and, where YouTube served them, its captions. Times are mm:ss in the video. Uplink's design follows these patterns where they fit (DESIGN.md).

Channels: [Matt Palmer](https://www.youtube.com/@mattpalmer), [@grok](https://www.youtube.com/@grok). Most useful single videos: [Getting started with Grok Bot](https://youtu.be/jwogmXNt7o4), [Grok Bot 101](https://youtu.be/CODaDnHwe2M), [Grok Bot 201](https://youtu.be/rOVomdnWhjQ), [Grok Bot 1.0](https://youtu.be/kjlDV-vf0-A), [What's new Nov 2](https://youtu.be/Ytf8Vl3bTfQ), [Ship Code at Scale with Grok Bot and Cursor](https://youtu.be/lpi-9F5KJJo).

## What every review agreed on

1. **The sidebar is calm and has three row states.** Nothing for a calm row; a small blue dot at the right edge for unread; an orange dot and orange preview starting "Waiting for you: …" for an agent blocked on you. No counts, no chips, no per-row times in most builds. The preview line is the agent's latest result in its own words ("Repro'd the checkout crash.", "Inbox triaged. 3 unread."), so it doubles as status. A running agent shows a green dot on its avatar, or the avatar animates. ([Getting started](https://youtu.be/jwogmXNt7o4), [Ship Code at Scale](https://youtu.be/lpi-9F5KJJo))
2. **Rows are roomy.** A 32 to 40 px avatar, the name, one gray preview line, about 56 px per row. Up to three pinned agents sit on top as large avatars (about 56 px) with a role caption; the rest are grouped under small gray headers that collapse to a count ("Engineering 3", "Hidden Bots 4"). A floating "↓ More unreads" pill appears when unread rows are scrolled away.
3. **One status phrase instead of spinners.** While an agent works, its small avatar sits beside a 2 to 4 word present-tense phrase with a shimmer ("Writing launch routes", "Getting it done"); the reply replaces it, and the sidebar preview shows the outcome in the past tense. ([Grok Bot 201](https://youtu.be/rOVomdnWhjQ))
4. **Events are one gray centered line.** "Created routine ⏱ Morning briefing", "Messaged Cursor Product Expert", "Message from Fix checkout crash". Agent-to-agent talk folds into "3 messages with A and B", which expands on click. ([Grok Bot 201 27:44](https://youtu.be/rOVomdnWhjQ?t=1664))
5. **Decisions are answered inside the message, then shrink to a receipt.** Questions are lettered rows (A, B, C) with "Type your own answer" or a gray "Something else" last; one click answers. While a question is open the composer placeholder reads "Type your answer, or talk about something else". Once answered, the card folds to the question plus the chosen answer with a check, or "Dismissed". Action cards carry a state chip that moves from amber "Action needed" to "✓ Saved", "Sent", "Merged". ([Getting started](https://youtu.be/jwogmXNt7o4), [Ship Code at Scale](https://youtu.be/lpi-9F5KJJo))
6. **Approvals name the rule and can become rules.** "Allow <agent> to <action>?", one plain line, "Details ⌄" with the exact command, "Paused by your Ask first rule · <rule>" with "Edit rule", then "Deny" and a black "Allow once ⌄" whose menu holds "Always allow". A card raised by background work expires ("• Expired", "Always allow next time"). The presenters: the app is "smarter about what needs to be approved". ([Grok Bot 1.0 05:52](https://youtu.be/kjlDV-vf0-A?t=352), [Trusting Grok Bot 01:11](https://youtu.be/B-2yDmwe2uw?t=71))
7. **Coding runs are cards and rows, not logs.** A run card: harness icon and name, bold task title, one line of scope, "Status Running" pill, branch, "Open in <tool> ⌄" and "View PR"; it updates in place to "✓ Finished" with "± 33 files +1756 −2". The agent's right pane lists runs under "Coding" (spinner, then "Finished" or "Ready for review · #3"). ([What's new Nov 2 01:48](https://youtu.be/Ytf8Vl3bTfQ?t=108), [Ship Code at Scale](https://youtu.be/lpi-9F5KJJo))
8. **The thread header is a centered title pill.** Avatar and name at top center, "Share" or a lock at the right, icon-only actions. Clicking the pill opens a right panel (about 290 to 360 px) with tabs "Details | Library | Computer" (or "Members" in a group): routines as plain schedule rows with toggles ("Weekdays at 8:00 AM"), a live thumbnail captioned "<agent>'s screen" or "Computer asleep", and a "Suggested" list of up to three setup steps that disappear when done.
9. **Results are pages, not long text.** A report arrives as a card (thumbnail, title, the word "Page", "Open"); it opens over the thread with the composer still at the bottom, so you change it by asking. Pages lead with a one-line blocker callout and an "At a glance" row of big numbers. ([Pages in Grok Bot](https://youtu.be/Pgytha2bLDM))
10. **Short messages with bold lead words.** An agent's turn is 1 to 3 short bubbles; reports are bullets starting with a bold phrase ("The count is 24, not 30.", "Next:"), the outcome first, and one judgment call named once with how to undo it.
11. **Quiet by default.** One notification, to the device in use; the phone waits about two minutes while you're at the desktop ("You're at your Mac, so your phone holds this for 2 minutes."). Notify only when an agent finishes or needs input. Quiet hours are a sentence typed to the agent. A webinar summary: a bot "should only ping you about [what] really matters and put the rest in a daily roundup."
12. **Unread inside a thread.** A thin "NEW" rule above the first unseen message and a floating "↓ 2 new messages ×" pill when you're scrolled up; nothing when everything is read.
13. **Settings are a modal with a short left menu** (General, Computer, Permissions, Vault, Usage & Billing, Updates) and grouped cards: a bold label, one gray line, the control at the right. Permissions holds plain-English rules: "Always Ask" and "Allow without Asking", each with "Add Rule".
14. **The agent's color.** In a one-to-one thread, your bubbles, the send and voice buttons and the "NEW" rule take the agent's color; a setting turns it black.

## Applied in Uplink so far

Sidebar rows with faces, a "Waiting:" line only on an agent that waits, dots instead of counts, the Inbox as a conversation with decision cards answered in place and lettered choices, events and folds as centered lines, the title pill, the pill composer with the arrow, "You're all caught up", Settings as a modal, and the two placeholders above. See DESIGN.md.

## Not yet in Uplink

The agent right panel (Details, Library, Computer), coding-run cards that update in place, approvals that become rules ("Always allow", needs a room API change), expiring background approvals, receipts that keep answered cards in a thread with a state chip, pages as deliverables, the "NEW" rule styling and unread pills, the agent's color on your bubbles, notification hold-off between devices, and group headers with counts and a Hidden group.
