The board, machines, and adding an agent

The last screens before building: a project's board, a machine's settings with access levels and slots, and the flow for adding an agent, where its owner builds its character from the system. Everything here follows DESIGN.md and the glossary. Press Send answers to Claude when you're done.

1

A project's board

Reef Runner's tasks. The research notes said "status comes from the runs' state, so nobody drags cards", so the columns are the run states and cards move on their own. Click A run changes to watch one move.

What a card shows

Each card above shows the task's owner and its runs, each run as the agent's character or the harness's mark with its state. The alternative is a count ("3 runs").

When the owner lowers the limit

Above, lowering the most open access drops running terminals to the new limit right away. The bridge enforces it on the machine either way (ADR 0002).
