8

What to call that view

The glossary defines Inbox as "the runs waiting on one person". Inbox Zero needs a name for the whole view, including Working and Done.

9

How finished work leaves the view

In the prototype, Done rows wait for you to settle them, and one shows an auto-settle rule ("Settles now: PR merged"). Settled work stays findable in its project and in search.

10

What one row is

In the prototype, "Fix double jump on ledges" is a task row with its two runs inside, and "Try Vite 7 on the website" is a run nobody filed into a task (an unfiled run).

11

Whose work the view shows

"Mine" means tasks you own and runs you drive. The prototype's Team tab adds teammates' rows with their names on them.

12
